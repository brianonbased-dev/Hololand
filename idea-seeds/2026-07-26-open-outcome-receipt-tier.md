Title: Open-outcome receipt tier for live-model headless runs

What might be valuable: The HoloScript headless experiment plan schema (`holoscript.headless-experiment-plan.v1`) requires `expected.finalPublicState` and exact schedule/observation/action counts declared before the run, and `buildHeadlessExperimentReceipt` hard-asserts the final state matches — so the v2–v4 source-run receipt family can only seal runs whose outcome is known in advance. Live model turns (Model Village Phase 1 and any future live-agent experiment) need a sibling tier: an "attested, not pre-determined" receipt that seals the frozen inputs (sources, manifests, ceilings, assignment hashes) up front, appends outcome ledgers as they happen, and distinguishes byte-identical re-execution claims (deterministic sub-engines) from attest-only claims (provider responses replayed from sealed custody). MV-B1's certification + ModelTurnReceipt lane (Hololand `scripts/model-village-adapter-runtime.mjs`) is the consumer that proves the need. A related small recoverable: a read-only audit open for the sealed custody store (`scripts/model-village-custody-store.mjs`) so preserved/frozen copies can be verified without appending to the access log.

Why it is not being pursued now: MV-B1 scoped to the adapter seam + custody drills inside Hololand; the receipt tier is a HoloScript substrate change (packages/cli + packages/engine HeadlessExecutionLedger) that deserves its own designed slice (MV-B2+ dependency), not a rider on this one.

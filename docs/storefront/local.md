**Status:** local merchandising SSOT. This repo is the **worlds store**.
Language work belongs in HoloScript. The vehicle people ride is
`.ai-ecosystem` ([handbook](C:/holo-dev/ai-ecosystem/docs/handbooks/holon-storefront.md)).

Public GitHub V1 of *this* door is gated in
[`github-v1-gate.md`](./github-v1-gate.md). Do not rewrite `README.md` as
TikTok-complete while the consumer loop is still unbuilt.

## What we put in the window

People **inhabit** worlds. Agents **prove** those worlds came from
HoloScript source: intent → `.holo` → validate → execute/render → live
interaction → receipt.

The consumer product on that substrate (founder D.114): easy to use,
build digital worlds, play at the park or in the living room. That is
the *product*. The builder-proof loop is the *engine*. Do not revive the
retired R3F / package-garden platform to fake the product.

## Greeter

1. **Who is shopping?** A person who wants to be in a world, an agent
   building one for them, or a teammate on Frontier.
2. **Which job?** Open/inhabit, create from intent, map a real place,
   twin a machine, Frontier shard.
3. **What’s in hand?** Nothing, a share link, a `.holo`, a headset, a
   filmed room.

Send compiler/parser work to HoloScript. Send QR admission to
`HoloScript/docs/storefront/admit.md`. Send “scan the repo” nowhere —
that is not this store.

## Result

The accepted Receipt Loom Courtyard now renders four deterministic physical
surface families authored by HoloScript:

- aged timber with directional grain, fiber wear, and knot compression;
- lime plaster with mineral mottling, trowel grain, and sparse hairline cracks;
- hand-split slate with offset courses, cleave variation, and retained rain sheen;
- rain-darkened basalt with multi-scale mineral grain, shallow pitting, and
  locally varied clearcoat.

Each surface owns four 128×128 channels: albedo, tangent-space normal,
roughness, and clearcoat. The sixteen generated byte arrays are SHA-256-bound in
the `.holo` source, independently regenerated in Node, hashed again inside
Chromium, and required to match before the witness can pass.

The material overlay inherits the sealed Art Convergence A courtyard without
editing its source, checker, or immutable manifest. Its presentation bridge
extracts the accepted A browser application from the exact pinned checker bytes
and wraps only `MeshPhysicalMaterial` construction.

## Visual review

The final image was visually inspected at its native 1600×900 resolution and
compared with both Art Convergence A and the Stormglass Commons concept.

Accepted gains:

- cottage beams, doors, rails, and planter boxes now read as directional wood
  rather than flat brown primitives;
- plaster and slate separate through surface response as well as color;
- roof courses carry visible per-tile variation;
- basalt and perimeter stones respond to rain with restrained specular breakup
  rather than a uniform glossy scalar;
- the scene retains the locked indigo/cyan/warm-copper hierarchy and the Loom
  remains the visual anchor.

Two rejected iterations informed the final tuning. The first made basalt normal
and clearcoat response too strong, producing false bright seams. The second
removed those seams but flattened the courtyard. The accepted third pass uses
boundary-free multi-scale mineral grain and a lower normal/clearcoat response.

This is a real material upgrade over A, but it remains visibly below the concept
in geometric density, building count, vegetation mass, weathering, atmosphere,
resident quality, and warm practical-light coverage.

## Provenance route

```text
HoloScript material overlay
  -> deterministic local texture synthesis
  -> Node SHA-256 verification
  -> immutable Art Convergence A browser application
  -> Three.js MeshPhysicalMaterial bindings
  -> Chromium SHA-256 verification
  -> WebGL2 / ANGLE D3D11 render
  -> durable hero image and witness receipt
```

No texture, model, or HDRI network request is used. JavaScript owns only
deterministic synthesis, validation, binding, capture, and receipt production;
the `.holo` file owns recipes, seeds, palettes, scale, bindings, hashes, budgets,
and truth boundaries.

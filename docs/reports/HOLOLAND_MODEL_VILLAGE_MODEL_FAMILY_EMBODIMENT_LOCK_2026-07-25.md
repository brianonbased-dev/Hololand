## Identity architecture

A public model-family identity and a live research identity cannot occupy the
same uncontrolled presentation layer. The lock therefore defines three
explicit profiles:

| Profile | Visible presentation | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| `village_story_unblinded` | Public family name and detachable family mantle | The independent-project disclosure is visible. No live adapter or exact model revision is implied. |
| `research_live_blinded` | `Resident 01` through `Resident 06` with neutral mantles | Family name, family mantle, provider route, adapter assignment, exact revision, condition, performance, and outcome stay outside resident observations and live observer presentation. |
| `research_replay_postlock` | Receipt-bound public family identity | Typed verification must admit the terminal commitment, family-binding receipt, and unblinding receipt. Missing, malformed, mismatched, or unverified evidence fails to the neutral profile. |

## Character construction

The production design specifies one shared construction with separable identity
layers:

1. **Shared body and rig:** the same skeleton, base material family, neutral
   clips, locomotion, interaction affordances, and logical tools.
2. **Family mantle:** a detachable HoloLand-authored garment, motif, pattern,
   glyph, and plain-text label used only in an admitted public/post-lock
   profile.
3. **Civic-role kit:** a neutral research-persona prop and village
   responsibility that remains independent of model family and grants no
   exclusive capability.

Family distinction is carried by the mantle, not by face, body type, apparent
demographics, movement quality, voice, competence, or access. Copied provider
logos, wordmarks, official mascots, and trade-dress mimicry are outside the
accepted art path. Civic role and role prop are excluded from the family mantle
manifest and public-embodiment digest. No authored resident rig or detachable
mantle is yet integrated as a runtime asset.

The public pattern/glyph vocabulary is also mechanically role-neutral. The
checker rejects public mantle identifiers containing any neutral role or prop
stem (`water`, `repair`, `tool`, `bridge`, `seed`, `hearth`, `host`, `courier`,
`map`, `path`, `ledger`, `witness`, and the complete locked source list).

## Research claim boundary

The family roster does not change the experiment.

- The current study design contains three sealed adapters, not six.
- Those adapters rotate across six personas in four conditions per seed block.
- Three seed blocks produce twelve planned village-runs.
- Exact provider routes, model revisions or local file hashes, adapter
  assignments, and condition identities remain sealed by the experiment
  protocol.
- A public name or family mantle is not evidence that the named family's model
  executed in any research seat.
- Live models and the full six-resident lifecycle remain unobserved until their
  separate Phase 1 gates close.

The research appearance digest and public embodiment digest remain separate.
The public embodiment digest uses only public embodiment ID, public family
name, family ID, agent surface, model-family label, and detachable mantle
manifest/pattern/glyph/accent. It excludes research alias, seat, persona, civic
role, role prop, silhouette, seat glyph, and neutral accent. Adapter permutation
must preserve the neutral research appearance. Only typed verification of a
family-binding and unblinding receipt after terminal commitment may create a
post-lock display association; it does not retroactively alter what any
resident observed. That verification is required by source policy but remains
unobserved as runtime execution.
